# Andrew Chingos

PhD Researcher in Climate Dynamics  
School of Earth & Environmental Sciences, University of St Andrews

I study the physical processes that control how the Earth's climate responds to warming, with a particular focus on tropical climate dynamics, land–atmosphere interactions, and extreme temperatures. My research combines climate theory, observations, and Earth System Models to better understand and constrain future climate change.

This website contains information about my research, publications, outreach, and open-source projects.
